Hush

Private one-to-one chat and voice calls. No accounts, no history, nothing stored.

  • End-to-end encrypted. Messages and audio travel directly between the two browsers over WebRTC (DTLS / SRTP).
  • The server is blind. It only introduces the two browsers. It never sees your messages, your audio, or the room key.
  • Verified. Both sides prove they hold the same secret link before anything is sent.
  • Ephemeral. Close the tab and the conversation is gone.

You'll get a secret link. Send it to one person through a channel you trust.

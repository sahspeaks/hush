Hush

Private group chat, voice and video calls for up to 6 people. No accounts, no history, nothing stored.

  • End-to-end encrypted. Messages, audio and video travel directly between browsers over WebRTC (DTLS / SRTP).
  • The server is blind. It only introduces the browsers to each other. It never sees your messages, your audio or video, or the room key.
  • Verified. Everyone proves they hold the same secret link before anything is sent to them.
  • Ephemeral. Close the tab and the conversation is gone.

You'll get a secret link. Send it to the people you want to talk to, through a channel you trust.
